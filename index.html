Essencia Artisan Bakery is a downtown Yakima bakery and chocolaterie for fresh breads, pastries, espresso, and a short lunch counter. Public listings point to Facebook. This concept puts the counter, the hours, and a phone call on one page.

The shop

A community bakery in a brick building.

A published shop description says Essencia opened in 2003 to give Yakima a community artisan bakery, with seasonal produce from the Yakima and Kittitas valleys. That account names a later group of Ryan Low, Alana Lautensleger, and Jean Scheid. Confirm current ownership before reprinting it. Facebook calls it a bakery and chocolaterie at 4 N 3rd Street, with outdoor seating and in-store pickup.

What people come back for

Reviews name almond and chocolate croissants, ham and cheese croissants, Danish rye, Palouse and Bordelais loaves, sweet potato curry soup, and iced Americanos. Lunch posts have included Reuben, turkey cucumber, spicy pork, ficelles, panini, and Friday musubi.

Items rotate. No fixed price list was published.

How to order

In-store pickup is listed. Delivery is listed as unavailable on several directories. Call for a larger bread or pastry order.
